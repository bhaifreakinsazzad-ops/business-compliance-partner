# Customer Journey Map — BCP Three-Pillar Ecosystem (V7.3)

## Journey 1: "I Want To Learn" → Courses (`/courses`)
1. **Entry:** Visitor lands on Homepage → sees Hero (`Explore Courses`) or Flagship Card `01 · Courses` (`আমাদের সকল কোর্সসমূহ`) or Desktop/Mobile Nav `Courses`.
2. **Discovery:** Browses priority tracks (`Income Tax`, `VAT & Mushak`, `e-GP & Tender`, `E-commerce`, `Company Secretarial`) on Homepage or `/courses`, filters by category or keyword.
3. **Course Evaluation (`/courses/[slug]`):** Inspects target audience, learning outcomes, interactive `SyllabusExplorer`, and verified metadata (no fabricated dates/fees).
4. **Conversion & Cross-Pollination:**
   - Enquires directly via pre-filled Emerald WhatsApp CTA or `/consultancy?pillar=COURSES`.
   - Discovers **Relevant Professional Services** (e.g., e-TIN/BIN filing support) and **Companion Books** (`এই কোর্সের সাথে সহায়ক প্রস্তাবিত বইসমূহ`) directly on the detail page.

## Journey 2: "I Need Professional Assistance" → Services (`/services`)
1. **Entry:** Visitor selects `02 · Services` (`আমাদের সকল সেবাসমূহ`) from Header, Mobile Drawer, Flagship Cards, or Hero Slide.
2. **Triage:** Switches seamlessly across **Professional Business Services** (`/services`), **Licensing & Registration** (`/licensing`), and **Expert Consultancy** (`/consultancy`) from the Services Gateway.
3. **Service Evaluation (`/services/[slug]`):** Reviews scope of assistance, official authority link (`AuthorityNote`), interactive `DocumentReadinessWidget`, and 4-step workflow.
4. **Conversion & Cross-Pollination:**
   - Launches pre-populated Bangla WhatsApp message (`+880 1521-537864`) or submits `/consultancy?pillar=SERVICES`.
   - Discovers **Related Courses** to upskill internally and **Reference Books** on the same compliance topic.

## Journey 3: "I Want Practical Knowledge & Publications" → Books (`/books`)
1. **Entry:** Visitor selects `03 · Books` (`আমাদের সকল বইসমূহ`) from Header, Mobile Drawer, Flagship Card 3, Connected Ecosystem, or Closing CTA.
2. **Storefront Exploration (`/books`):** Filters across 9 compliance domains (`Tax`, `VAT & Mushak`, `e-GP`, `RJSC`, `Accounting`, `E-commerce`, `Customs`, `Labour Law`, `Business Management`), seeing honest pre-launch status (`প্রকাশনা আসন্ন`) with hardcover mockups.
3. **Interactive Preview (`/books/[slug]`):** Explores `BookPreview3D` (front/back cover flip + reduced-motion static fallback), topic outline, and target readers.
4. **Interest Registration & Cross-Pollination:**
   - Registers pre-publication interest via WhatsApp or `/consultancy`.
   - Navigates directly to **Related Courses** and **Related Professional Services** in the exact domain.

## Journey 4: Guided & Search-First Visitors
- **Unified Search (`#unified-search` & `Ctrl+K` QuickSearchDialog):** Type any keyword (e.g., `VAT`, `RJSC`, `e-GP`) → filter with `All | Courses | Services | Books` tabs → jump directly to matching detail pages.
- **Business Solution Finder (`#solution-finder`):** Select goal + business type + stage → deterministic recommendation routes straight to the matching Course, Service, Licensing, Book, or Consultancy destination.
- **Connected Ecosystem (`ConnectedEcosystemShowcase`):** Toggle `Income Tax`, `VAT & Mushak`, `e-GP & Tender`, `Smart RJSC`, or `E-commerce & DBID` to view the linked Course, Service, and Book path in one screen.
